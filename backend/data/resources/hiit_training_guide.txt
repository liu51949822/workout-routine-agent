High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) Guide
=============================================

What is HIIT?
HIIT alternates between short bursts of intense exercise and brief recovery periods. This training method is highly effective for improving cardiovascular fitness, burning calories, and building endurance in minimal time.

Benefits of HIIT:
- Time efficient (15-30 minute workouts)
- Boosts metabolism for hours after exercise (EPOC effect)
- Improves both aerobic and anaerobic fitness
- No equipment needed for many protocols
- Highly adaptable to any fitness level

Work-to-Rest Ratios:
Beginner: 1:3 (20 seconds work, 60 seconds rest)
Intermediate: 1:2 (30 seconds work, 60 seconds rest)
Advanced: 1:1 (40 seconds work, 40 seconds rest)
Elite: 2:1 (40 seconds work, 20 seconds rest)

Sample HIIT Protocols:

TABATA (4 minutes)
- 20 seconds maximum effort
- 10 seconds rest
- Repeat 8 rounds
- Choose one exercise (burpees, sprints, mountain climbers)

15-Minute Full Body HIIT:
Round 1 (5 min):
- Jumping Jacks: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Burpees: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- High Knees: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Mountain Climbers: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec

Round 2 (5 min):
- Squat Jumps: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Push-ups: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Alternating Lunges: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Plank to Down Dog: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec

Round 3 (5 min):
- Star Jumps: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Bicycle Crunches: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Speed Skaters: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec
- Tuck Jumps: 45 sec
- Rest: 15 sec

Low-Impact HIIT Modifications:
For those with joint concerns:
- Replace jumping with stepping variations
- Use resistance bands for added intensity
- Focus on controlled tempo movements
- Marching in place instead of running

20-Minute Low-Impact HIIT:
- Step-out Squats: 40 sec
- Rest: 20 sec
- Modified Burpees (no jump): 40 sec
- Rest: 20 sec
- Standing Oblique Crunches: 40 sec
- Rest: 20 sec
- Reverse Lunges: 40 sec
- Rest: 20 sec
Repeat 3 rounds

Recovery Guidelines:
- Don't do HIIT more than 3-4 times per week
- Allow 48 hours between sessions for full recovery
- Include proper warm-up (5 min) and cool-down (5 min)
- Stay hydrated before, during, and after
- Listen to your body - scale intensity as needed

Heart Rate Zones:
Zone 1 (50-60%): Recovery/Warm-up
Zone 2 (60-70%): Fat burning, easy effort
Zone 3 (70-80%): Aerobic, moderate effort
Zone 4 (80-90%): Threshold, hard effort
Zone 5 (90-100%): Maximum, all-out effort

HIIT should alternate between Zone 4-5 (work) and Zone 1-2 (recovery).

Sample Weekly Schedule:
Monday: HIIT (20 min) + Core (10 min)
Tuesday: Active Recovery (walking, stretching)
Wednesday: Strength Training
Thursday: HIIT (15 min)
Friday: Mobility and Flexibility
Saturday: HIIT (25 min) + Light Cardio
Sunday: Complete Rest
