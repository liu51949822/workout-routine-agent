Mobility and Flexibility Training Guide
=======================================

Understanding the Difference:
- Flexibility: The ability of a muscle to lengthen passively
- Mobility: The ability to move actively through a range of motion with control

Both are essential for injury prevention, performance, and daily function.

Key Mobility Areas for Athletes:

1. Hip Mobility
The hips are crucial for squatting, running, and martial arts. Limited hip mobility leads to compensation patterns in the lower back.

Hip Mobility Drills:
- 90/90 Stretch: 60 sec each side
- Deep Lunge with Rotation: 30 sec each side
- Hip Circles: 10 each direction, each leg
- Pigeon Pose: 60 sec each side
- Frog Stretch: 60 sec

2. Thoracic (Upper Back) Mobility
Important for overhead movements, posture, and rotational sports.

T-Spine Drills:
- Cat-Cow: 10 reps, slow and controlled
- Thread the Needle: 8 reps each side
- Foam Roller Extension: 30 sec
- Open Book Stretch: 10 reps each side

3. Ankle Mobility
Essential for deep squatting and athletic performance.

Ankle Drills:
- Banded Ankle Mobilization: 10 reps each
- Elevated Calf Stretch: 30 sec each
- Ankle Circles: 10 each direction

4. Shoulder Mobility
Required for overhead pressing, pull-ups, and throwing movements.

Shoulder Drills:
- Wall Slides: 10 reps
- Shoulder CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations): 5 each direction
- Pass-Throughs with Stick: 10 reps
- Dead Hangs: 30 sec

Daily Mobility Routine (10 minutes):
1. Cat-Cow: 1 minute
2. World's Greatest Stretch: 1 minute
3. Hip 90/90: 2 minutes (1 each side)
4. T-Spine Rotation: 2 minutes
5. Ankle Mobility: 1 minute
6. Shoulder Circles: 1 minute
7. Deep Squat Hold: 2 minutes

Pre-Workout Dynamic Warm-Up (5-7 minutes):
- Arm Circles: 30 sec
- Leg Swings (front/back): 20 each leg
- Leg Swings (side to side): 20 each leg
- Inchworms: 5 reps
- Walking Lunges with Twist: 10 steps
- High Knees: 30 sec
- Butt Kicks: 30 sec

Post-Workout Static Stretching (10 minutes):
Hold each stretch 30-60 seconds:
- Standing Quad Stretch
- Standing Hamstring Stretch
- Hip Flexor Stretch
- Piriformis Stretch
- Chest Stretch (doorway)
- Lat Stretch
- Tricep Stretch
- Neck Stretches

Foam Rolling Protocol:
Use slow, controlled rolls - spend extra time on tight spots.
- IT Band: 1 minute each side
- Quadriceps: 1 minute
- Hamstrings: 1 minute
- Glutes/Piriformis: 1 minute each side
- Upper Back: 1 minute
- Lats: 1 minute each side
- Calves: 1 minute each

Jiu-Jitsu/Grappling-Specific Mobility:
For guard work and submission defense:
- Hip Escape Drills (shrimping)
- Bridge and Roll Practice
- Granby Rolls
- Technical Stand-ups
- Combat Base Transitions

These movements improve:
- Hip flexibility for guard play
- Spine mobility for escapes
- Shoulder flexibility for arm defense
- Overall body coordination

Weekly Mobility Schedule:
- Daily: 5-10 min basic mobility routine
- 2x/week: Focused foam rolling session (15 min)
- Post-workout: Static stretching (10 min)
- Weekly: One dedicated mobility session (20-30 min)
