Introduction (0:00-1:30)
Welcome back to the channel. Today we're diving deep into core training and why most people are doing it wrong. The core isn't just your abs - it's your entire midsection including your obliques, lower back, hip flexors, and glutes. They all work together as a unit.

The Anti-Movement Principle (1:30-4:00)
Here's the key insight: your core's primary function is to RESIST movement, not create it. This is called anti-extension, anti-rotation, and anti-lateral flexion. When you're doing a plank, you're training anti-extension. When you do a Pallof press, you're training anti-rotation.

Top Core Exercises Ranked:

1. Dead Bug (Beginner)
- Lie on your back with knees at 90 degrees
- Extend opposite arm and leg while maintaining flat back
- The key is keeping your lower back pressed into the floor
- 3 sets of 10-12 reps per side

2. Pallof Press (All Levels)
- Use a cable or resistance band at chest height
- Press away from body and hold, resisting rotation
- Keep hips square and core braced
- 3 sets of 10-12 reps per side

3. Plank Variations (All Levels)
- Standard plank: 30-60 seconds
- Side plank: 20-30 seconds each side
- Plank with arm reach: adds instability
- Focus on quality over duration

4. Ab Wheel Rollout (Advanced)
- Start from knees if beginner
- Roll out maintaining neutral spine
- Go only as far as you can control
- 3 sets of 8-10 reps

5. Hanging Leg Raises (Advanced)
- Hang from pull-up bar
- Raise legs with control, not momentum
- Lower slowly to maximize tension
- 3 sets of 8-12 reps

Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- Doing hundreds of crunches (low effectiveness, high volume)
- Ignoring the obliques and lower back
- Using momentum instead of control
- Training core when fatigued from other exercises

Programming Core Training:
- Train core 2-3 times per week
- Can be done at end of workouts or as standalone sessions
- Progress by adding time, reps, or resistance
- Include both static holds and dynamic movements

The 10-Minute Core Circuit:
1. Dead Bugs: 45 seconds
2. Plank: 45 seconds
3. Side Plank Left: 30 seconds
4. Side Plank Right: 30 seconds
5. Bird Dogs: 45 seconds
6. Pallof Hold: 30 seconds each side
7. Hollow Body Hold: 30 seconds

Rest 15 seconds between exercises, repeat circuit 2x for intense session.

Conclusion (12:00-13:00)
Remember, core strength transfers to everything you do - from heavy lifts to everyday activities. Train it with intention, focus on anti-movement patterns, and you'll see real improvements in your performance and posture.
